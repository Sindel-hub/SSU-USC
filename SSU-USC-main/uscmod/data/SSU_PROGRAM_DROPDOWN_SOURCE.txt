Samar State University undergraduate program dropdown

Source basis:
- Official Samar State University article dated September 3, 2025: "SSU achieves 100% COPC compliance across all undergrad programs."
- The source includes programs from multiple SSU campuses. For this USC portal, the student/officer account dropdown intentionally keeps only the Catbalogan/main-campus colleges used by this deployment:
  - College of Education
  - College of Arts and Sciences
  - College of Nursing and Health Sciences
  - College of Engineering
  - College of Industrial Technology
- SSU Mercedes Campus and SSU Paranas Campus are intentionally excluded from the account-registration dropdown.

The July 22, 2026 SSU article reports Bachelor of Public Administration and BS Environmental Science undergoing CHED program/institutional monitoring and validation. They are not included in this dropdown yet because the 2025 COPC list is the cleanest officially published complete program-to-college mapping. Add them only after the school confirms their final college mapping and operating status for this portal.
