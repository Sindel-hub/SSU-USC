System Administrator: responsibilities and limits
Appointment: Pending USC and appropriate university office approval. The displayed account role does not appoint an individual or office.
The designated administrator manages authorized accounts, role assignments, access, records, configurations, monitoring, documented maintenance, troubleshooting and reviewed updates. Technical maintenance belongs to the designated university IT support or formally authorized maintainer, whose appointment is also pending.
The administrator does not independently determine USC policy, certify student eligibility, approve financial reports or change official academic records. Use authorized USC lists and approval references. University offices remain responsible for official verification.
  1. Verify written authorization before provisioning an account or assigning access. Use User Directory and the validated student import process.
  2. Assign the approved office, administration period, term dates and minimum required module permissions.
  3. Review activity records and access periodically. Resolve synchronization failures before advising an officer to sign in again.
  4. At turnover, complete the outgoing assignment, check access revocation, then assign the successor. Never share passwords or rename an old account to a new officer.
  5. For incidents, record the time, affected module and non-sensitive error reference; restrict affected access, notify the authorized university contact and preserve evidence.
  6. Test maintenance in staging, document changes and recovery, and obtain explicit approval before pushing or deploying. Keep credentials private.
Historical records and activity logs cannot be edited or deleted here. Retention and exceptional corrections require an approved university procedure; do not invent or backdate missing historical accomplishments.
